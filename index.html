01 / BUSINESS CONTEXT

Why do we need an HR AI Assistant?

Organisations maintain HR policies in documents. Employees need quick and reliable answers, but finding the right information manually can be time-consuming.

❌ Traditional Approach

Employees depend on manual document searches and HR clarification.

  • Search lengthy policy documents
  • Find the correct policy section
  • Contact HR for routine questions
  • Spend time interpreting policy language

✓ Proposed AI Approach

Use a conversational knowledge assistant powered by RAG.

  • Ask questions using natural language
  • Retrieve relevant policy information
  • Generate contextual answers
  • Display supporting source documents

07 / PRODUCT ROADMAP

From MVP to Enterprise AI

The current application establishes the RAG foundation. The following phases can improve quality, governance and capabilities.

PHASE 01 · CURRENT

Working RAG MVP

TXT ingestion, chunking, embeddings, ChromaDB, semantic retrieval, Groq generation and Streamlit.

PHASE 02 · ENHANCE

Advanced RAG

PDF and DOCX ingestion, improved chunking, metadata filtering and RAG evaluation.

PHASE 03 · ENTERPRISE

Enterprise HR Assistant

Authentication, role-based access, policy versioning, audit logs and HR escalation.

PHASE 04 · AGENTIC AI

HR AI Agent

Approved workflow initiation, employee request handling and human-in-the-loop decisions.
